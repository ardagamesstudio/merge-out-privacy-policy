Privacy Policy

Android puzzle game · Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Merge Out (the “Game”), an Android puzzle game published under the name Arda Games Studio by the individual developer and data controller Arda Alex Akca, handles information. For questions or privacy requests, contact contact.ardagames@gmail.com.

The Game does not require an account or sign-in. It stores gameplay information locally. The intended advertising-enabled Google Play release may use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for eligible players aged 18 or older. Those services are not used by the Game's advertising flow for younger players or players who do not provide their age. This policy describes the intended release configuration. If the distributed version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated.

Information saved on your device

The Game stores information needed for gameplay and preferences, including campaign levels and completions, Daily puzzle progress, selected themes and unlocks, Sound and Haptics preferences, tutorial and feature state, and age-category and advertising-eligibility settings. This information is stored in the Game's app-specific storage, using Unity PlayerPrefs. The Game does not use a publisher-operated player account or cloud-save server.

Unity PlayerPrefs are not encrypted by the Game itself. Android backup and device-transfer features may copy or restore app preferences and progress depending on the final app configuration and your device settings. A device backup may persist after local app data is cleared. The developer does not operate the Android backup service.

Age choices and younger players

The Game is intended for players aged 6 and older. On first use, it asks players to select one of four broad choices: 12 years old or younger, 13–17 years old, 18 years old or older, or Prefer not to say. No full date of birth or player account is required for this selection. The selected category is saved locally. This is a self-reported choice and does not independently verify a player's age.

The “12 years old or younger” choice is a single category and does not distinguish users under 6 from users aged 6–12. Older saved Under-6 profiles and other protected or unresolved profiles are handled conservatively for advertising.

  • 18 or older: players with an advertising-eligible adult profile may enter Google's consent and advertising flow, subject to applicable privacy choices and technical eligibility checks.
  • 12 or younger, 13–17, Prefer not to say, unanswered, or protected profiles: the Game's controlled advertising flow does not initialize UMP/Google Mobile Ads or request or show advertisements.

Selecting an age category is not consent to advertising or other data processing. The restriction above describes the Game's application-controlled flow and does not by itself guarantee that the operating system or unrelated services process no data.

Advertising and Google services

For eligible adults, the Game may use Google Mobile Ads to request interstitial advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform to obtain privacy requirements and display applicable consent messages. The Game requests an ad only when its own eligibility checks and UMP's ad-request status permit the request. If an ad is unavailable or not permitted, the Game can continue without showing it.

Interstitial opportunities are designed for breaks after completing campaign levels and selecting Next, not for startup, active gameplay, or Daily puzzles. The first opportunity is no earlier than after four completed campaign levels; after an advertisement is shown, further opportunities require at least three newly completed levels and at least 180 seconds. This describes the intended scheduling rules, not a guarantee that an ad will be displayed.

According to Google's Mobile Ads Unity data disclosure, the SDK automatically collects and shares certain information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. The categories Google describes include:

  • IP addresses, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • product interactions, including app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostics and performance information, such as launch time, hangs and energy use; and
  • device or other identifiers, including App Set ID and, where applicable, additional device or account-related identifiers.

The planned Android configuration restricts permissions for Android Advertising ID and certain Android AdServices identifiers. This restriction does not mean Google receives no device identifiers. The precise processing can depend on the final SDK, device, privacy choices and advertising configuration.

UMP may communicate with Google's services to determine the privacy messages applicable to an adult and to manage consent or privacy-choice status. Google processes information it receives under its applicable terms and privacy disclosures. For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google's explanation of information from partner apps.

Consent and privacy choices

For an eligible adult, the Game requests updated information from UMP and presents a consent message when required by Google's configuration. An age selection alone never indicates consent. Available advertising may depend on privacy choices, local law and Google's ad-serving settings.

The Privacy & Ads entry appears in Settings for all age categories. For eligible adults, it can show Google's privacy-options form when UMP indicates that it is required and available; otherwise it provides relevant privacy information. For younger, unknown-age or protected profiles, it presents local privacy information without starting the Game's advertising SDK flow.

You may contact the developer at the email below about privacy rights or questions. Android and Google also provide their own privacy controls.

Recipients and third-party services

Where the adult advertising flow runs, Google receives information through Mobile Ads and UMP as described above and in Google's policies. Google handles information received through its services under its published privacy terms and applicable data-protection arrangements. The current release plan does not include a separate advertising mediation provider or publisher-operated gameplay analytics or account backend. We will update this policy if the release adds such services.

If you email the developer, your email address and the contents of your message are received and processed to respond to your inquiry and any applicable obligations. The email provider processes correspondence to deliver the email service. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive information.

Purposes, legal bases and international processing

The Game saves gameplay progress and settings on the device to provide the puzzle game and remember choices you make. The developer does not intentionally transmit these local saves to a publisher-operated server. Where the local processing involves personal data, its purpose is to provide the gameplay functionality requested by the player.

For eligible adults, UMP is used to check applicable privacy requirements and obtain choices where required. Where consent is legally required for advertising-related processing, the Game presents the applicable UMP choices before requesting ads. Different technical, security, fraud-prevention and ad-measurement activities may have different legal bases under the applicable rules and Google's services. The publisher and Google may have different legal responsibilities for their respective processing. You can contact the developer for information about the legal bases applicable to processing under the developer's responsibility.

If you contact us by email, we use the information you send to answer your request or to comply with legal obligations relating to that request. Depending on the situation and law, this processing may be necessary to respond to your request, pursue our legitimate interest in providing support, or fulfil legal obligations. We do not require you to email us to play.

Google and the providers of email or webpage hosting services may process information in countries other than your own. Their published privacy information describes their activities and available international-transfer protections. For information about Google's international transfers and safeguards, see Google's privacy information linked below. You can also ask us about transfers of information under the developer's responsibility using the contact details below.

Retention, deletion and privacy rights

Game progress, settings and the saved age category generally remain in local app storage until changed or removed. You can remove local records by clearing the Game's storage or uninstalling the app, but doing so also removes local gameplay progress. Depending on Android backup and device settings, an existing backup may retain or restore some data. There is no player account to delete and no publisher-operated server copy of gameplay saves in the reviewed Game.

Google manages the data it receives through its services according to its policies, controls and applicable requirements. Clearing local game storage does not automatically delete information held by Google or copies in a device backup. The developer does not promise a fixed retention period for information controlled by Google. If you contact us by email, the message may be kept for as long as needed to address the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of correspondence we hold, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where relevant, or lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. Email contact.ardagames@gmail.com to ask about information held by the developer. We will review requests received at this address in accordance with applicable law. We may ask for information needed to establish which records we control, and we cannot directly delete data held by Google or Android backup providers. Google's own privacy channels are relevant for Google-held information.

Security

Local PlayerPrefs are not encrypted by Unity. Google states that information collected through Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). This does not independently establish the transport security of every UMP or platform-related data flow. This statement about Google Mobile Ads does not establish the security of every other third-party data flow. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.
